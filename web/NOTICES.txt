The readings and gloss extracts are filtered and aggregated for this benchmark.
Romanization is generated locally. Kana readings may have multiple dictionary
senses; a displayed gloss is not an inferred intended meaning. The benchmark
vocabulary snapshot is pinned for reproducibility, not a live dictionary service.
JMdict-derived extracts retain the above license. No endorsement is implied.

TUBELEX Japanese frequency data
Source: https://github.com/naist-nlp/tubelex
Pinned source revision: 7cb5fb36add76b83a266d1967536e1a1d3faa513
Frequencies are matched to eligible dictionary readings, filtered for ambiguity,
and aggregated into kana metrics. These transformations are ours.
